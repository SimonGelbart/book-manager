# Personal EPUB collection

The user's downloaded AO3 reading collection, its organization, and their own reading history and notes.

## Language

**Collection**:
The user's personal set of AO3 works and downloaded EPUB copies, together with their reading history and notes.

**AO3 work**:
A piece of writing published as one work on Archive of Our Own, distinct from any downloaded EPUB copy.
_Avoid_: Book, story in project documentation when this specific concept is intended.

**EPUB copy**:
One downloaded EPUB file containing an AO3 work. Several EPUB copies can correspond to the same AO3 work.

**Duplicate indicator**:
A visible indication that more than one EPUB copy is associated with the same AO3 work.

**Reading status**:
The user's record of whether an AO3 work in the collection has been read, shared across its EPUB copies.

**Personal note**:
Text the user writes to record their own thoughts about an AO3 work, shared across its EPUB copies.
