# Personal EPUB collection

The user's downloaded AO3 reading collection, its organization, and their own reading history and notes.

## Language

**Collection**:
The user's personal set of downloaded AO3 EPUBs managed for organization and choosing something to read.

**Reading status**:
The user's record of whether an item in the collection has been read.

**Personal note**:
Text the user writes to record their own thoughts about an item in the collection.
